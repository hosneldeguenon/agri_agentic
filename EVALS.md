## Unfixed failure

### A18: Sowing in the dry season (15 Dec, Borgou maize) is refused (no advisor window for Borgou yet)

Expected: ok=false or a warning field mentioning season/out-of-season. Observed: `{"ok": true, "expected_irrigation_mm": 122, "season_check": {"status": "not_covered", "source": null, "note": "No confirmed sowing-window data for this department and crop: the season is NOT checked."}}`.

Cause: sowing windows are only encoded where an extension advisor confirmed them (so far Zou, maize: March-April and August-September; `sowing_windows.py`). For every other department/crop the season is NOT checked and the rain-scenario generator (`engine.generate_scenarios`) draws a 120-day rainy-season profile indexed from day 1 of the season, ignoring the calendar sowing date. A 15 December sowing in Borgou therefore still gets a normal-looking plan; the output says `season_check: not_covered` but the call is not blocked.

What I would try next: collect windows per department and crop from advisors (one interview per zone), store them with their source, and make `plan_irrigation` refuse when a zone has no data and the month is in the dry season; align scenario days to calendar dates using the M2 profiles' start dates.
